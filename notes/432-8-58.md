# [[432,8,58]] — weight-10 metacyclic 2BGA distance-first

## Direction & hypothesis

Target: the weight-9plus / any-weight cell at moderate n, distance-first
(small k, large d). The opportunity map (fieldnote 2026-08-08, Opp D) noted
that in the k in [4,16] window the board's best distance was capped around
d=34 at n=348 ([[348,4,34]]) and d=32 at n=376 ([[376,6,32]]), and that a
structured code with d >= 40 at k >= 8, n ~ 400-470 would stand alone on a
Pareto axis. Non-abelian lifted-product / two-block group-algebra codes were
the natural search space: the board's weight-8 odd-k lane comes from the same
metacyclic family, and its distance-value under deep search was already
calibrated in the B/C routes (`fieldnotes/2026-08-09*`).

## What was searched

2BGA over metacyclic groups C_n x| C_k (n from 6..39, k from 2..7, semidirect
multipliers r with r^k = 1 mod n) and lifted products over dihedral D_m, with
support sizes (4,4) -> weight 8 and (5,5) -> weight 10; n = 2|G|, swept to
n <= 700. 1560 constructions passed CSS; roughly 945 had k in [4,16]; all
were screened with the kit's randomized GF(2) coset-leader search
(`surrogate.distance_rand`) at 600 trials and ranked by kd^2/n. 310 survivors
with d_screen >= 12 were persisted (spec + fingerprint) to
`research/candidates/oppD/d_survivors.json`; the top survivors by screen K
were then re-run at 12k-20k trials to strip screen inflation.

## Evidence trail

The screen is an upper-bound search and materially over-estimates distance on
this family, so a two-stage ladder was used. The submitted code:

- screen (600 trials): `[[432,8,70]]`, K = 90.7
- deep confirm (12,000 trials, X side first): lightest X logical 60, lightest
  Z logical 58 -> `[[432,8,58]]`, K = 62.3
- packaged via `make_submission` (witness embedded, `confidence:
  upper_bound`) and re-run through the trusted gate
  `verify/validate_candidate.py`: `passed: true`, cell
  weight-9plus x unrestricted, `board_advancing: true`.

Candidates that collapsed between screen and deep confirm (same metacyclic
family, all weight 10):

- `[[432,12,70]]` K 136 -> d 24 (K 16) at 8k trials
- `[[420,12,66]]` K 124 -> d 30 (K 25.7) at 12k trials
- `[[432,8,68]]` (fp 1cef...) K 85.6 -> d 36 at 12k trials

Final claim: witness-backed upper bound d <= 58 (Z side; X <= 60), not an
exact certificate. Both witnesses are embedded in the submission and checked
by the verifier.

## Dead ends

- High-rate weight-10 targets at k >= 12 collapsed hardest; the surviving
  candidates that held deep confirmation all sat at k = 6-8, i.e.
  rate ~ 0.02, exactly the distance-first niche.
- The k=8 candidates at n=468 (`[[468,8,70]]`, `[[468,10,62]]`) screened
  above 80 K but did not survive 15k-trial re-confirmation.
- RIS screen inflation is severe in this family: top-of-screen K values
  routinely dropped 3-6x between 600 and 12k trials.
