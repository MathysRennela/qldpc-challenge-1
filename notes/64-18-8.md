(github.com/QEC-pages/2BGA-codes, commit 403d194c) to fill a gap on the
weight-8 × unrestricted board. The database entry for GAP SmallGroup(32,21)

## What was searched

Single reconstruction from the public database. The code is a generalized
bicycle (2BGA) over the group G = SmallGroup(32,21) with:

- a(x) = 1 + g2 + g3 + g17
- b(x) = 1 + g7 + g22 + g31

where g_i denotes the i-th group element in GAP's ordering. The construction
follows the database's specification exactly; no search or mutation was applied.
