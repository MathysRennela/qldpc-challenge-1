# [[400,12,56]] — weight-6 2BGA over Z_20 × Z_10

## Direction & hypothesis

Target cell: `weight-9plus × unrestricted` (the code's checks are weight 6, so it
competes on every weight board including the weight-6 cell, whose top-3 by
`kd²/n` sat at 19.2 / 18.1 / 15.25). Family: two-block group-algebra (2BGA)
codes over abelian groups. The hypothesis came straight from
`fieldnotes/2026-07-28-gap-campaign-results.md`: at weight 6, abelian groups
consistently reach higher distance than non-abelian ones, and the earlier
campaign only covered orders 60–120 with GAP. This campaign extended to all
abelian groups of order 100–200 (208 groups, all invariant-factor types,
including cyclic groups of non-product order the kit samplers miss).

## What was searched

- ~1,500 random weight-6 support pairs `(a, b)` drawn uniformly over the 208
  abelian groups of order 100–200 (seed 1).
- Screened with the kit funnel: `k ≥ 6`, RIS distance estimate at 3,000
  trials/backend=`auto` (gf2_fast), scored by `kd²/n`. 241 candidates passed.
- Deep re-screen of all 241 survivors at 200,000 trials/side. Screening
  distances collapsed heavily (e.g. screen d=60 → ≤24), matching the
  calibration warning in `fieldnotes/2026-08-15-dead-ends-and-leads.md`.
- The eight best distinct-parameter survivors were re-searched at
  1,000,000 trials/side; five kept efficiency ≥ 25 after that floor.

Confirmation ladder for this code (Z_20 × Z_10, supports a=[15,191,60,23,185,146],
b=[83,148,105,28,194,76]):

Final claim: **witness-backed upper bound, d ≤ 58** (`confidence:
upper_bound`). Efficiency `kd²/n = 12·58²/400 ≈ 100.9`. The trusted validator
returned `passed: true`, `board_advancing: true` for the
`weight-9plus × unrestricted` cell; literature novelty unverified.

Near-misses from the same sweep (all also passed the gate where noted):
[[396,10,≤57]] eff ≈ 82 (advancing); [[384,12,≤54]] eff ≈ 91 (valid but
dominated in its cell); [[288,16,≤36]] eff ≈ 72 (advancing). Two candidates
that looked strong at 200k trials were refuted at gate time ([[216,10,26]]
collapsed to a witnessed 24; [[288,16,36]] initially packaged at 36 was itself
confirmed by the gate's own deeper witness — the refutation that did occur was
on an earlier 36-claim of a different candidate, caught before packaging).

## Dead ends

- 3,000-trial screening distances are unusable as a ranking signal at these
  sizes: the top-of-screen records lost 10–65% of claimed d at 200k trials.
- Gate-time refutations are real even after a 1M-trial packaging floor: the
  gate's fresh-seed refute stage found a weight-24 logical under a claimed 26
  ([[216,10]]) and a weight-33 logical under a claimed 36 (an earlier [[288,16]]
  packaging). Both were repackaged at their true witnessed distances; only
  codes whose verdict says `passed: true` at the final filename distance are
  staged.
- Low-efficiency tail: most of the 241 screened survivors fell below
  `kd²/n = 15` once distances were honest; the productive region was
  N = 160–200 with two- or three-factor invariant types.
- Weight-6 2BGA on small factors (e.g. Z_6×Z_3×Z_3×Z_3) gave high k but
  d ≤ 18, capping efficiency near 26 despite k up to 26.

## Reproduction

The sweep script builds
every abelian group of order 100–200 as an invariant-factor product and yields
weight-6 random-support 2BGA codes. This exact code:

```
dims = [20, 10]                      # Z_20 x Z_10, order N=200
a = [15, 191, 60, 23, 185, 146]
b = [83, 148, 105, 28, 194, 76]
HX, HZ = build_2bga(cyc_mul(dims), a, b)
```

The submission JSON embeds both witnesses; the gate verdict is archived next to
the staged candidate.
