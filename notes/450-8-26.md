# [[450,8,26]] — spectral-screened bivariate bicycle on Z_15 x Z_15

## Direction & hypothesis

Target cell: `unrestricted` x `weight-6` (both check blocks weight 3). The
hypothesis: the spectral theory of semisimple BB codes (arXiv:2608.27565)
makes the logical dimension k exactly computable from the check polynomials'
common zeros on the root grid, without building any matrix, so a random
weight-3 sweep can be screened ~50-120x cheaper than build-and-rank and the
survivors witness-searched in depth. Odd grids only (the semisimple case).

## What was searched

4000 random weight-3 (A, B) pairs over 7 odd grids (Z_9xZ_9, Z_9xZ_15,
Z_15xZ_15, Z_7xZ_15, Z_15xZ_9, Z_9xZ_21, Z_7xZ_9), seed 20260831. Funnel:
exact spectral k >= 8 (191 pass) -> witness search (250 trials) with
d >= 6 (38 pass) -> gate. Three gate-passers, all on Z_15 x Z_15; this is
the one that survived deep re-verification.

## Evidence trail

Ladder for this code: 250-trial screening upper bound 36 -> packager
20k-trial witness dX <= 32, dZ <= 30 -> 2M-trial accelerator pass tightened
dX to 26. Final claim: **witness-backed upper bound, d <= 26**
(dX <= 26, dZ <= 30; per-side witnesses in the submission JSON); not
exact-certified. The validation gate (verify + independent refutation)
passed, and the CLI's own 2M-trial pass could not beat 26.

The two sibling gate-passers collapsed under the same deep search and were
not submitted: one fell from d <= 26 to [[450,8,24]] (dominated by the
board's [[330,8,24]]), the other from d <= 30 to the same [[450,8,26]]
claim as this code. Calibration data from the sweep (40 stage-1 codes):
the strip-form colon-ideal lower bound (arXiv:2608.27565 Thm 91) has
median gap 13 to the witnessed upper bound on random weight-3 codes — it
kills candidates but does not rank them.

## Dead ends

- The colon-ideal floor as a ranking signal: floor median 2, within 2 of
  the upper bound in only 2/40 cases. Keep it only as a kill-criterion.
- Random unit multiples (Thm 140 preserves k and floors): 600 products,
  zero weight reductions — units only inflate weight.
- Region census (20000 samples): 80% of random weight-3 odd-grid codes
  have k = 0; the exact-k screen is what pays, not region sampling.
- Low-trial screening bounds inflate: every survivor's d dropped under the
  2M-trial pass (36 -> 26, 26 -> 24, 30 -> 26). Confirm at depth before
  believing any screening number.

## Tools

Model: GLM 5.3 Flash (Zed agent). numpy-only kit plus a purpose-built
spectral screener: GF(2^e) evaluation of the check polynomials on the root
grid Z_l x Z_m (e = ord_lcm(2) <= 20, primitive polynomials verified at
use), exact k = 2|Z_a ∩ Z_b| per arXiv:2608.27565 Thm 81. No gf2_fast; a
single laptop CPU, roughly 20 minutes of compute for the whole sweep plus
the CLI's deep witness passes.

## Reproduction

Build H_X = [A | B], H_Z = [B^T | A^T] over the group algebra of
Z_15 x Z_15 (monomial x^a y^b = S_15^a (x) S_15^b; index = i*15 + j):

- A = x^2 y^14 + y^7 + x^13 y^3  (terms (2,14), (0,7), (13,3))
- B = x^4 + x^9 y^8 + x^8 y^10   (terms (4,0), (9,8), (8,10))

n = 2*15*15 = 450, k = 8 (exactly 2|Z_a ∩ Z_b| = 8 common zero grid
points), check weight 6. Any GF(2) circulant construction reproduces this;
the polynomials above are the complete specification.
