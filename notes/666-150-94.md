# [[666,150,94]] mutated designed-divisor generalized-bicycle code

## Direction & hypothesis

This search targeted the unrestricted, weight-9plus high-rate cell around the prior cyclic designed-divisor construction over `Z_333`. The goal was to preserve `k=150` and check weight 30 while removing the low-weight logical that refuted the earlier `d<=80` submission.

## What was searched

The mutation campaign generated XOR combinations of cyclic shifts of the two original degree-75 designed-divisor supports. Each pair was expanded into the cyclic generalized-bicycle CSS matrices, checked for commutation and rank, and filtered to maximum row weight 32. The selected supports have 14 terms and 16 terms, so every check row has weight 30.

The selected code is:

```text
A = [30, 82, 84, 102, 131, 140, 210, 221, 228, 239, 282, 304, 320, 329]
B = [6, 20, 49, 60, 62, 65, 78, 173, 186, 237, 239, 246, 247, 263, 283, 304]
```

For `N=333`, the construction is

```text
H_X = [circ(A) | circ(B)]
H_Z = [circ(B)^T | circ(A)^T]
```

where `circ(S)` is the binary `333 x 333` circulant whose row `i` has support `(i+j) mod 333` for `j` in `S`.

## Evidence trail

The exact matrix expansion and logical witnesses are included in `codes/666-150-94.json`. The packaged witnesses have weights 94 on the X side and 97 on the Z side, giving the honest witness-backed claim `d<=94`. This is an upper bound, not an exact-distance claim. The direct verifier also found and retained the weight-94 X witness at seed `1155639336`.

The trusted candidate validator passed the structural verifier, witness checks, deduplication checks, and frontier check. Its 8,000-trial RIS refutation search found no lighter logical at seed `21160816`. The validator reported this as advancing the `weight-9plus × unrestricted` board cell; literature novelty remains unverified.

## Confirmation limits

A BP+OSD decoder cross-check was attempted locally, but the decoder is not practical at this blocklength in the available environment: a single decode takes roughly 1.8 seconds and the documented 200,000-trial run did not complete within the available budget. The CI RIS gate remains the final independent check for this submission.
